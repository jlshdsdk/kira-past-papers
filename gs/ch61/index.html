$$\eta=\frac{dx}{dp}\cdot\frac{p}{x}=-3p^3 \Rightarrow \frac{dx}{x}=-3p^2\,dp.$$

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}nx^n=\frac{x}{(1-x)^2}=\frac{20/21}{(1/21)^2}=420.$$
